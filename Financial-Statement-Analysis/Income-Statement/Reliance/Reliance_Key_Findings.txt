RELIANCE INDUSTRIES – INCOME STATEMENT KEY FINDINGS
FY2025 vs FY2026

1. Revenue from operations increased by 9.75%.

2. Total income increased by 10.67%, slightly faster than revenue growth.

3. Total expenses increased by 10.02%, slightly slower than total income growth.

4. Profit for the year increased by 17.77%, significantly faster than revenue growth.

5. Net profit margin improved from 8.30% in FY2025 to 8.90% in FY2026, indicating improved profitability.

6. Purchase of stock-in-trade increased by 14.88%, faster than revenue growth.

7. Excise duty increased by 19.51%, significantly faster than revenue growth.

8. Finance costs increased by 11.51%, faster than revenue growth.

9. Other expenses increased by 12.97%, faster than revenue growth.

10. Employee benefit expenses increased by 6.16%, slower than revenue growth.
