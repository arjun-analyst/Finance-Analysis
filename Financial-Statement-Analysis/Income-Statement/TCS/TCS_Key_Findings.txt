TCS — Income Statement Key Findings
FY2025 vs FY2026

1. Revenue from operations increased by 2.83%.

2. Total expenses increased by 1.39%, which was lower than revenue growth of 2.83%.

3. Profit for the year increased by 10.62%, faster than revenue growth.

4. Net profit margin improved from 22.37% in FY2025 to 24.06% in FY2026, an improvement of approximately 1.70 percentage points.

5. Employee benefit expenses increased by 4.51%, faster than revenue growth.

6. Equipment and software licence expenses decreased by 69.43%.

7. Finance costs increased by 59.89%, the highest percentage increase among the major expense categories.

8. Other expenses increased by 12.60%, faster than revenue.

TCS Annual Report FY2025-26
